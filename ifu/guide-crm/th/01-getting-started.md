## Sign in

:::split crmth/login_code, crmth/m_login | Enter the 6-digit code on a computer (left) · the sign-in screen on a phone (right).
1. Open MMH CRM in **Chrome**, **Edge** or **Safari**.
2. Enter your company e-mail (**@manimedicalhanoi.com** or **@mani.inc**) ▸ **Send sign-in code**.
3. Open your mailbox, copy the **6-digit code** ▸ enter it ▸ **Sign in**.

The session stays active for **30 days** on that device. If the code does not arrive within one minute, check the **Junk / Spam** folder, then use **Resend code**.

The same sign-in is used by MMH Report Hub, Training Hub and the KPI pages; signing in once on a device is enough.
:::split-end


## Install the app

:::split crmth/install, crmth/m_more | Install on a computer (left) · on a phone: More ▸ Install app (right).
Installing gives MMH CRM its own icon and window, and it opens faster.

- **Computer**: click **Install app** on the top bar ▸ **Install**.
- **Android**: **More** (bottom bar) ▸ **Install app** ▸ **Install**.
- **iPhone / iPad**: open the CRM in **Safari** ▸ **Share** ▸ **Add to Home Screen**.

After installing on an iPhone, sign in once more inside the installed app.
:::split-end

## Screen layout on a computer

:::split.wide crmth/home | The work calendar with the left menu and the top bar.
**Left menu**, grouped by purpose:

- **Overview**: Dashboard · KPIs & Targets
- **Customers**: Customer List · Key Stakeholders
- **Activities & Plans**: Activity Log (customer tasks) · Daily Tasks (other tasks) · Presentations · Event · Monthly Plan
- **Sales Result**: New account & SKU
- **Tools & Reports**: Weekly Meetings · Submit Report

**Top bar**: MMH Calendar · Quick search · fiscal year · **?** Help · Reload · Install app · Mentions · What's new.
:::split-end

## Screen layout on a phone

:::split.even crmth/m_cal, crmth/m_today | Calendar (left) · Today (right) with the bottom bar.
The **bottom bar** gives one-tap access to the most used screens:

| Button | Opens |
|---|---|
| **Calendar** | Your tasks by day and week |
| **Customers** | Customer list and profiles |
| **Today** | Today's tasks, due-soon and overdue items |
| **New acc.** | New account & SKU cases |
| **More** | Every other function, Install app, Help |

The round **＋** button adds a task from any screen. On **Today**, each customer task has **Take photo** and **Report result**.
:::split-end

## Quick search

:::split crmth/search | Ctrl + K: type a few letters to find a customer or a task.
Press **Ctrl + K** (Mac: **⌘ + K**) or click **Quick search**. Type part of a customer name, a province or a task. Click a result to open it.
:::split-end
