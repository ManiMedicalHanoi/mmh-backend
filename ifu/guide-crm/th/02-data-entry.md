## Add a customer task

:::split crmth/task_new, crmth/m_task_new | The customer task form on a computer (left) and a phone (right).
1. **Activity Log** ▸ **Calendar** ▸ click **＋ Add task** under the day (phone: the round **＋**).
2. Choose **Field visit** (customer task).
3. **Account**: click the box and pick the customer. Not in the list ▸ **＋ New site** (see *Customers and contacts*).
4. **Type task**, **Sales Process** and **Product group** are filled in from your last task with that customer — check them.
5. **Type action**: Visiting, Phone call, Online meeting, **Co-visiting with distributor**, **Event**, **Product test**.
6. **Plan**: what you intend to do ▸ **Save to Google Sheet**.

The form closes immediately. With no signal, the entry waits on the device and is sent automatically later. Plan next week's tasks before sending the weekly report: the report takes **next week's plan** from them.
:::split-end


## Report the result

:::split crmth/task_result, crmth/m_task_result | Task Result with 4 boxes (computer) · Report result on a phone.
After the meeting, open the task (phone: **Today ▸ Report result**):

1. Click **CBC — person met** and choose the contact you met. The **Person met** line is required.
2. Complete the **4 boxes** of Task Result:
   - **Actions Taken** — what you did;
   - **Current Situation** — the customer's situation;
   - **Customer Feedback** — what the customer said;
   - **Opportunities / Next Steps** — the next step.
3. Set **Task Status = Completed** ▸ **Update to Sheet**.

Each box has a **Voice** button for dictation. A completed task with an empty box is not saved.
:::split-end

:::split crmth/task_list | List view: every task of the period with its status and missing information.
**Activity Log ▸ List** shows your tasks by day. Red labels such as **missing CBC** mark tasks that still need information. Click a card to complete it.

:::tip When the task ended on an earlier day
If you complete a task from a previous day, choose **This task ended on: the selected date / today / another date**. The date is saved exactly as chosen.
:::
:::split-end

## Co-visits, events and product tests

Use the **Type action** of a customer task to record the activities counted in the team KPIs:

| Type action | Use it when | Counted in |
|---|---|---|
| **Co-visiting with distributor** | You visit a customer together with a distributor sales representative (e.g. DDI) | Co-visits (C4-01) and days with co-visits (C4-03) |
| **Event** | You meet the customer at a seminar, workshop or exhibition | Customer history; the event itself is recorded under **Event** |
| **Product test** | The customer tests a MANI product | Product test results (sheet PRODUCT TEST, C11-03) |

## Other tasks

:::split crmth/other_keys | ＋ Key Task: choose one of the five key tasks of the month.
Office work and internal work are recorded in **Daily Tasks** (sheet OTHERS TASK), never as customer tasks.

1. **Daily Tasks** ▸ **＋ Key Task**.
2. Choose one of the **five fixed key tasks of the month**:
   - **Reports & Plans** — weekly / monthly reports, plans, CRM updates;
   - **Fanpage & Consulting** — online questions and advice;
   - **Event Enrolment** — inviting customers, confirming attendance;
   - **Training & Research** — product study, demo preparation, internal training;
   - **Others** — anything else.
3. Add a **sub task** with a clear name, the dates and the result. Move the progress bar or set the status.
:::split-end

:::split crmth/other, crmth/m_other | Daily Tasks on a computer (left) and a phone (right).
Customer tasks are also mirrored into OTHERS TASK automatically. They are hidden in Daily Tasks by default; tick **Also show field visit tasks** to see them.
:::split-end

## Business trips

:::split crmth/trip_propose, crmth/m_trip_propose | Business trip request on a computer (left) and a phone (right).
1. **Activity Log ▸ Calendar ▸ Propose a business trip** (or click a day ▸ **Business trip request**).
2. Enter the **dates**, **destination**, **co-traveller**, **purpose**, **expected result**, **schedule** and **estimated costs in THB**.
3. **Send proposal**.

The request is saved to the **Thailand Dental Business trip** file with Approval Status *Not Yet*, and the approval e-mail is sent to the Director, CC the HOD. The manager approves in the Google Sheet.
:::split-end

:::split crmth/trip_card | After approval: open the trip on the calendar and fill in the trip report.
After the trip, click the trip on the calendar ▸ fill in **Key Activities**, **Key Findings** and the next actions ▸ **Save report**. The report is saved in the trip folder and e-mailed to the managers.

Before approval you can still **Edit** or **Delete** the request.
:::split-end

## Events

:::split crmth/event | The Event list: events of the month with status and participants.
**Event** shows every seminar, workshop and exhibition of the **Dental Event Report** file by month: upcoming and done, target and actual participants.
:::split-end

:::split crmth/event_form | The Add event form.

1. **＋ Add event**.
2. Enter the **date**, **type of event**, **target product**, **topic**, **KOL / PIC**, **partner** and **place**.
3. When MMH leads the session, write **MMH** before the name in KOL / PIC (e.g. *MMH Man*). This is how MMH-led events are counted.
4. **Plan status** and **Action status** ▸ **Save to Google Sheet**.

After the event, open it again and fill in the **Event review** and the **actual participants**.
:::split-end

## Customers and contacts

:::split crmth/cust_new | ＋ New account: the customer and its first contact in one form.
**Customer List** shows each account with its province, PIC, activities and targets.

To add a new customer: **＋ New account** ▸ enter the **name**, **account type**, **province**, **area**, address ▸ the **first contact** (name, phone, job title) ▸ **Save territory**. The customer code is created automatically.
:::split-end

:::split crmth/cust_profile, crmth/m_cust | Customer profile: timeline, contacts and account plan (left) · Customer list on a phone (right).
Click a customer to open the **360° profile**: timeline of all tasks, key contacts, account plan (ADP) and activity in the last 12 months. Use **Add CBC** to add another contact and **Log activity** to add a task for this customer.
:::split-end
