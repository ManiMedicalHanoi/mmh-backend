## Dashboard

:::split crmth/dash, crmth/m_dash | Dashboard on a computer (left) and a phone (right).
**Dashboard** ▸ choose the month.

- **Sales**: distributor sales of the month and to date against target, sales by month, and each sales KPI against its own target.
- **Customers**: visits by month, customers by type, customers that need a new visit.

**Refresh** reloads the latest figures from the CRM and the turnover file.
:::split-end

## KPIs & Targets

:::split crmth/kpi, crmth/m_kpi | KPIs & Targets on a computer (left) and a phone (right).
**KPIs & Targets** lists your KPIs with **weight**, **target**, **actual** and **%** for the **month**, the **quarter** or the **fiscal year to date**.

Colours follow the KPI file: grey below 90 %, blue 90–99 %, yellow 100–120 %, green above 120 %. Actual figures come from the CRM sheets and the KPI file; you do not type them here.
:::split-end

## Calendar views

:::split.wide crmth/cal_month | Month view: field visits, other tasks, business trips, events and training on one calendar.
**Activity Log ▸ Calendar ▸ Month** shows the whole month. Use the chips at the top to show or hide **Field visit**, **Other tasks**, **Business trip**, **Event** and **Training**, or to hide completed tasks.
:::split-end

## Good practice

| Do | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Enter the task **on the same day** and complete the 4 result boxes | Writing several days at once at the end of the week |
| Choose the customer from the list; add a **New site** only when it does not exist | Creating the same clinic twice with a different spelling |
| Record office work in **Daily Tasks** under the monthly key task | Using an office or "business trip" account in customer tasks |
| Use **Co-visiting with distributor** for every joint visit | Recording a co-visit as a normal visit (it is not counted) |
| Send the weekly report before the deadline, after checking the quick view | Sending without completing the week's tasks |
