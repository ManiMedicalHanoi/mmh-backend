## Weekly report

:::split crmth/report, crmth/m_report | Submit Report ▸ Weekly report on a computer (left) and a phone (right).
1. **Submit Report** ▸ **Weekly report**.
2. Check the period (**This week**). Next week's plan is taken from the tasks you planned for next week.
3. **Open quick view** to read the exact e-mail that will be sent: customer tasks, other tasks and the plan.
4. **Send me a test** to check, then **Send officially to the team**. A PDF is attached automatically.

Sending times are recorded automatically. Before sending, make sure every customer task of the week is **Completed** with the 4 result boxes, or still planned with a reason.
:::split-end

## Monthly plan

:::split crmth/monthly | Monthly Plan: the plan and the result of each item side by side.
**Monthly Plan** holds one item per type of work and month.

1. Choose the month ▸ **＋ Add item** (or **Generate all frames for this month**).
2. Write the **PLAN** at the start of the month: focus areas, key accounts, focus products.
3. At month end, write the **RESULT** in the same item ▸ **Save to Sheet**.
4. **Send monthly report** sends the month by e-mail.
:::split-end

## Monthly KPI report

:::split crmth/kpi_report | Monthly KPI Report (form MMHSOP_009-FM01) with the e-mail preview.
1. **KPIs & Targets** ▸ **Send KPI report** (or **Submit Report ▸ KPI report**).
2. Choose the month. For each KPI with an actual figure, write the **reason achieved / not achieved**.
3. Write the **actions for next month**, highlights, difficulties and the support you need.
4. Check the preview ▸ **Send monthly KPI report**. The Word form FM01 is attached.

The quarterly report (FM02) is sent from **Quarterly KPI report** in the same way.
:::split-end

## Deadlines

| Report | Deadline |
|---|---|
| **Weekly report** | 23:59 on the last working day of the week |
| **Monthly KPI report (FM01)** | Day **05** of the following month |
| **Quarterly KPI report (FM02)** | Day **05** of the first month of the next quarter |
