## About this guide

This guide shows the Thailand Dental sales team how to use **MMH CRM** in daily work. It follows the order in which you work:

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| **1 · Getting started** | Sign in, install the app, find your way around on a computer and on a phone |
| **2 · Data entry** | Customer tasks, other tasks, business trips, events and ROI, customers and contacts, account tracking, map of Thailand |
| **3 · Reports** | Weekly report, monthly plan, monthly KPI report |
| **4 · Overview** | Dashboard, KPIs & Targets, calendar views |

Each page shows the **text on the left** and **real screenshots on the right**. Screenshots use **sample data**; names and figures are not real.

:::rule Three rules for clean data
1. **Customer task** = work with a customer, a distributor or a key opinion leader only.
2. **Office work** (reports, meetings, preparation) goes to **Other tasks**.
3. **Business trips** are requested and reported with **Propose a business trip**, not as customer tasks.
:::

:::tip Help inside the app
Click **?** on the top bar (phone: **More ▸ Help**) for step-by-step tours of each screen.
:::
