## What MMH CRM is

:::split.even crmg_en/home, crmg_en/m_today | The main screen: computer (left) and phone (right).
MMH CRM is the Sales team's digital notebook. Everyday work is done in one app:

- plan **field visits**, record the result right after the meeting, take photos on site;
- manage **accounts** and **customers** (contacts);
- enter **orders**, send them for approval, follow the approval;
- **other tasks**, **business trips**, **product presentations**, **monthly plan**;
- send **weekly / monthly / KPI reports**, view **KPI** and the **Dashboard**.

Each team has **its own Google Sheet file**. The app keeps no data of its own: when you click Save, the data is written straight into the right tab of your team's file (see Chapter 8).

:::split-end

## Install on an Android phone

:::split crmg_en/m_install_more, crmg_en/ill_and_prompt, crmg_en/ill_home | More ▸ Install app (left) · Chrome's install box (middle) · the MMH CRM icon on the home screen (right).
About 1 minute, using **Chrome**.

1. Open **manimedicalhanoi.github.io/MMH-CRM** in **Chrome** and sign in (section 1.5).
2. On the bottom bar, tap **More** ▸ **Utilities** ▸ **Install app**.
3. Chrome asks **Install app?** ▸ tap **Install**.
4. The **MMH CRM** icon appears on your home screen. From now on, tap it to open the CRM — no need to open Chrome.

:::note No install box at step 3?
Tap **⋮** at the top right of Chrome ▸ **Install app** (some phones show **Add to Home screen** ▸ **Install**). If you opened the link from Zalo, Messenger or Gmail, tap **⋮** ▸ **Open in Chrome** first.
:::
:::split-end

## Install on iPhone / iPad

:::split crmg_en/ill_ios_share, crmg_en/ill_ios_sheet, crmg_en/ill_ios_add | Safari's Share button (left) · Add to Home Screen (middle) · tap Add (right).
About 1 minute, using **Safari**. iPhone does not let web pages install themselves, so follow these steps:

1. Open **manimedicalhanoi.github.io/MMH-CRM** in **Safari** (scan the QR code with the Camera and tap the link).
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an up arrow) on the bottom bar. iOS 26: tap **•••** next to the address bar ▸ **Share**. iPad: the button is at the top right.
3. Scroll down, choose **Add to Home Screen** ▸ tap **Add**.
4. Open the app from the **MMH CRM** icon and **sign in once more** (iPhone keeps the app's sign-in separate from Safari).

:::tip The same guide is inside the app
Sign in to the CRM in Safari ▸ **More ▸ Utilities ▸ Install app**: the app shows the exact steps for iPhone, with a picture of each button.
:::
:::split-end

:::split.even crmg_en/m_install_ios, crmg_en/m_install_android | The in-app install guide: iPhone (left) · Android (right).
### The install guide inside the app

The **Install app** button recognises your device:

- **Android, computer (Chrome / Edge):** opens the browser's install box directly.
- **iPhone / iPad (Safari):** shows the 4 steps above.
- **Mac with Safari:** menu **File** ▸ **Add to Dock…** ▸ **Add** (macOS 14 or later).

When you open the CRM from the installed app, the **Install app** button hides itself.
:::split-end

## Install on a computer

:::split.wide crmg_en/ill_desk | Click the install icon at the end of the address bar ▸ Install (Chrome illustration).
About 30 seconds, using **Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**.

1. Open the CRM link and sign in.
2. Click **Install app** on the top bar, **or** the install icon (a screen with a down arrow) at the end of the address bar.
3. Click **Install**. The CRM opens in its own window.
4. Next time, open it from the **Start menu** (Windows) or **Launchpad / Dock** (Mac). Right-click the icon on the taskbar ▸ **Pin** for quick access.

:::note Firefox
Firefox cannot install web apps yet. Open the CRM in Chrome or Edge.
:::
:::split-end

## Sign in

:::split crmg_en/login, crmg_en/m_login | The sign-in screen on a computer and a phone.
The CRM shares one account with **MMH Report Hub** and **Training Hub**.

1. Enter your **company e-mail** (@manimedicalhanoi.com or @mani.inc) ▸ **Send code**.
2. Open the e-mail, take the **6-digit code** ▸ enter it in the app ▸ **Sign in**.
3. The app knows who you are, your team and your permissions. You stay signed in for **30 days** on that device.

| Role | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Sales (PIC)** | Your **own** tasks, customers, orders, KPI |
| **Team Leader** | Your whole team (the **Whole team** chip and each PIC) |
| **Managers** (HOD, Director, Admin) | Switch the team being viewed, approve orders / new accounts, statistics |

:::note No code received?
Check your Spam folder. Each code works once and expires after a few minutes. Still stuck: contact the Admin (Product Team).
:::
:::split-end

## Shared tools

:::split.even crmg_en/search, crmg_en/tour | Quick search Ctrl + K (left) · the step-by-step guide catalogue (right).
### Quick search and step-by-step guides

- **Quick search** [[key Ctrl+K]] (phone: the magnifier icon): type a few letters of a customer, task or product name.
- **Step-by-step guides** (the **?** button): pick a guide; the app opens the right screen, highlights the button to press and explains it. Guides are marked **New** / **Updated** when a feature has just changed.
:::split-end

:::split.even crmg_en/whats_new, crmg_en/deadline | What's new (left) · accounting document reminder (right).
### Notices and reminders

- **What's new**: appears when you sign in during the 7 days after each update. Tick **Don't show again** to hide it. Click the version number at the bottom of the left menu to see the full history.
- **Reminders**: accounting documents (14th, 15th, 28th, 29th), monthly KPI report (2nd, before 16:00), quarterly self-assessment (9th of the first month of the quarter, before 17:00).
- **Colour theme**: the palette on the top bar (phone: More ▸ Theme).
:::split-end
