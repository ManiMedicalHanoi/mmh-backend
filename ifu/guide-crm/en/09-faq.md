## Installing the app

**No install button or menu item?** The app may already be installed on this device: look for the **MMH CRM** icon in the app drawer, Start menu or Launchpad. Opened inside Zalo, Messenger or Gmail ⇒ open it again in Chrome (Android, computer) or Safari (iPhone). Firefox cannot install web apps.

**Do I need to update the app?** No. Each time the CRM has a new version, the app updates itself when opened. Not seeing the change ⇒ close the app completely and open it again. **What's new** lists the changes.

**Will installing lose my data?** No. Your data is in your team's Google Sheet, not on the device.

**Uninstall:** Android: press and hold the icon ▸ Uninstall. iPhone: press and hold ▸ Delete app / Delete bookmark. Computer: open the app ▸ **⋮** on the title bar ▸ Uninstall MMH CRM.

## Signing in

**iPhone asks me to sign in again after installing?** That is normal: iPhone keeps the app's sign-in separate from Safari. Sign in once inside the app.

**No 6-digit code?** Check Spam; use your company e-mail; each code works once.

## Saving data

**I clicked Save but don't see it in Google Sheet?** The app saves in the background, usually within seconds. Offline: the change waits on the device and is sent when you are back online. A red chip ⇒ click it to Retry.

**I edited the Google Sheet directly but the app has not changed?** Click **Reload** (phone: More ▸ Reload).

**Cannot set Completed?** Check: all 4 Task Result boxes and the Person met line; Customer feedback + Next action (Visiting); the date is not in the future.

## Orders

**A new order cannot be saved: "no care history"?** Click **＋ Add care history** inside the order form (section 5.3).

**No Send for approval button on an order?** The Order Type or the documents are missing.

**Editing an approved order?** Possible, but the whole order returns to Pending and must be sent for approval again.

## Reports

**Sent by mistake?** Send the correct one again; the statistics use the **latest send** in the period.

**"E-mail quota" error?** The system switches to a backup sending route automatically; if it still fails, wait a few minutes, send again and tell the Admin.

:::note Getting help
Contact the **CRM Admin (Product Team)** with a screenshot of the problem, your team name and the time it happened.
:::
