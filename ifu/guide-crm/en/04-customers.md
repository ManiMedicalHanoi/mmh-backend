## The account list

:::split crmg_en/cust, crmg_en/m_cust | Customer List on a computer (left) and a phone (right, as cards).
Left menu ▸ **Customers ▸ Customer List** (phone: the **Customers** button).

- Each row: name, customer code, type (Hospital, Clinic, University, Dealer, Distributor), province, PIC, number of contacts, number of orders.
- Search box and filters by type, province, PIC, FY opened.
- Click a row to open the **account profile**.

The **customer code** is generated by a formula in the file from the region + province + customer type; you do not type it.
:::split-end

## Add a new account

:::split.wide crmg_en/newacc_form | The Add new account form, with the first contact person.
Click **＋ New account** on the Customer List page, or directly inside the field visit / order form (what you have already typed is kept).

1. Enter the **account name**, choose the **Account type**, **Province** (Area fills in), address, PIC, opening date.
2. In **First contact (CBC)**: name, phone, job title, specialty. This person is saved straight away as a **Key Stakeholder** of the account.
3. Click **Save account**.

If the name matches an existing account (common for clinic names), the app lists the accounts with the same name ⇒ choose **Use this account** (the contact you entered is still saved to that account) or **Create new account**.
:::split-end

## The account profile

:::split.wide crmg_en/cust_profile | The account profile: timeline on the left, Objective & ADP status on the right.
The profile gathers everything about one account:

- **Timeline**: field visits, orders and presentations by date;
- **Objective & ADP status** (right column): group A–D, target / actual, Objective, Key action, latest quarterly review;
- contacts, sales by month and by product, the last visit date.

Click **Edit** to change the account details; changes are written to tab 1 and to the change log.
:::split-end

## Customers (Key Stakeholders)

:::split crmg_en/stake, crmg_en/m_stake | The customer contact list: number of interactions, last meeting, sales.
Left menu ▸ **Customers ▸ Key Stakeholders**: the contacts (CBC) of every account.

- Search by name, account, phone, job title; filter by province or event.
- Chips **Not yet contacted**, **Most interactions**, **Highest sales**.
- **＋ Add CBC**: add a contact to an account.

A contact is **added automatically** when you:

- enter the first contact while creating a new account;
- type a new **orderer** in the order form (Chapter 5, Vietnam teams);
- record **care history** with a person who is not in the list yet.

The app matches by name (ignoring accents) or phone number, so the same person is not added twice.
:::split-end

## ADP Key account

:::split crmg_en/adp, crmg_en/m_adp | ADP Key account: board by group A–D.
Left menu ▸ **ADP Key account**: the development plan for key accounts.

- For each account: group **A–D**, analysis, Objective, tactics, **Key action**, monthly target / actual;
- a **review each quarter** (Q1–Q4) and for the year;
- view as an ABCD board or a list.
:::split-end
