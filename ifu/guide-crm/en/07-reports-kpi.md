## Send a report

:::split crmg_en/report, crmg_en/m_report | Send report on a computer and a phone.
Left menu ▸ **Tools & reports ▸ Send report**.

1. Choose the type: **Weekly report**, **Monthly report**, **Monthly KPI report**.
2. Choose the period and recipients: **test send to yourself**, **official send to the team**, or **Download PDF**.
3. Preview ⇒ **Send**. The e-mail has a PDF attached and is sent from an @manimedicalhanoi.com address.

| Report | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Weekly report | Before 24:00 on the last working day of the week (company calendar) |
| Monthly KPI report | Before 16:00 on the 2nd of the next month |
| Quarterly self-assessment | Before 17:00 on the 9th of the first month of the next quarter |

Managers see who has or has not sent in **Report statistics**.
:::split-end

## Weekly meeting

:::split crmg_en/meet, crmg_en/m_meet | Weekly meeting minutes: 4 steps.
Left menu ▸ **Weekly meeting**: the team's meeting minutes, in 4 steps:

1. **Overview**: distributor sales, monthly KPI.
2. **Customers**: customers met, notes.
3. **Activities & plans**: field visits, other tasks, presentations, trips, ADP — each tab has its own note box.
4. **Sales results**: SKUs, orders.

Save the minutes ⇒ e-mail / download PDF; earlier minutes are in **History**.
:::split-end

## KPI and targets

:::split crmg_en/kpi, crmg_en/m_kpi | KPI & targets: one table per PIC.
Left menu ▸ **Overview ▸ KPI & targets**.

- One table per PIC: **Code · KPI · Weight · Target · Actual · %** and score; view by **Month / Quarter / Year to date**.
- % colours: **below 90%** grey · **90 – below 100%** blue · **100 – 120%** yellow · **above 120%** green.
- Targets come from the **MMH KPI FY68** file; Actuals are calculated from CRM data (visits, orders, new accounts, presentations…).
:::split-end

## Monthly KPI report and quarterly self-assessment

:::split.even crmg_en/kpi_report, crmg_en/kpi_se | Monthly KPI report (left) · Setting Expectation / quarterly self-assessment (right).
- **Monthly KPI report**: choose the month ⇒ preview your KPI e-mail ⇒ **Send**. Deadline: the 2nd, before 16:00.
- **Setting Expectation** (quarterly self-assessment): fill in the company Word template ⇒ **Download** or **Send e-mail** (counted as submitted). Deadline: the 9th of the first month of the quarter, before 17:00.
:::split-end

## Dashboard

:::split crmg_en/dash, crmg_en/m_dash | The Dashboard, Sales tab.
Left menu ▸ **Overview ▸ Dashboard**, 2 tabs:

- **Sales**: monthly columns against the target line, sales KPI per line, product groups, high-value customers.
- **Customers**: visits per month, customers by type, coverage by PIC, customers needing follow-up.

The **To do** box lists your open items: orders to handle, new account cases to fix, presentations without a report, overdue field visits, last week's report not sent.
:::split-end

## For managers

:::split.wide crmg/mgr_dash | The manager's Dashboard: comparison by PIC (Vietnamese interface).
- **Switch the team being viewed**: left menu (phone: More ▸ Team) — Dental · Surgical · Eyeless · Thailand Surgical · Thailand Dental.
- The Dashboard adds a **Comparison by PIC** table, orders by status / type, coverage by PIC.
- **Approve orders** (HOD), **approve new accounts**, **Report statistics** (Excel export: who sent on time, late, not sent).
:::split-end

## Where the data is saved

| Data | Saved in |
|---|---|
| Weekly meeting minutes | The team's CRM file ▸ **13. WEEKLY MEETING** (Thailand Surgical: 20. WEEKLY MEETING · Thailand Dental: WEEKLY MEETING) |
| KPI | The team's CRM file ▸ **9. TARGET & KPI** ⇒ pushed to [[sheet kpi | MMH KPI FY68]] |
| Report sending log | Hidden sheet **_MMH_REPORT_LOG** in the team's CRM file |
| Distributor sales | [[sheet turnover | MMH Turnover Report]] (read-only) |
