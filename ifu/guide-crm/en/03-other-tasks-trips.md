## Other tasks: fixed monthly key tasks

:::split.wide crmg_en/other | Other tasks: fixed key tasks each month, with tasks underneath.
Left menu ▸ **Other tasks** (phone: More ▸ Other tasks).

Every month, each salesperson has **fixed key tasks**; everything else is recorded as a **task** (sub-task) under them:

| Thailand teams | Vietnam teams |
|---|---|
| Reports & Plans MM/yyyy | Báo cáo và kế hoạch Tmm.yyyy |
| Fanpage & Consulting MM/yyyy | Trực fanpage và tư vấn Tmm.yyyy |
| Event Enrolment MM/yyyy | Tuyển sinh sự kiện Tmm.yyyy |
| Training & Research MM/yyyy | Đào tạo và nghiên cứu Tmm.yyyy |
| Others MM/yyyy | |

Sales cannot create other key tasks or rename them. Managers (Team Leader, HOD, Director, Admin) can still create freely.
:::split-end

## Add a task

:::split crmg_en/other_keys, crmg_en/m_other | Choose the month's key task (left) · Other tasks on a phone (right).
1. Click **＋ Add task** (or ＋ under a day on the calendar) ▸ **Other task**.
2. Choose the **key task** of the right month. Each key task has a short description and examples.
3. Enter the task name, start date, due date and status ▸ **Save**.

The screen updates at once; the app sends the changes to Google Sheet one by one in the background. A small chip on the top bar shows how many are still waiting.
:::split-end

## Propose a business trip [[teams d s e td]]

:::split crmg_en/trip_propose, crmg_en/m_trip_propose | The trip proposal form on a computer and a phone.
For Dental VN, Surgical VN, Eyeless VN and Thailand Dental. Thailand Surgical proposes trips through its own process, not in the CRM.

1. On the calendar click **Propose a trip** (or ＋ under a day ▸ Trip).
2. Choose the **destination**, **start / end dates**, who goes with you, the **purpose** and expected results.
3. Enter the **daily schedule** and **estimated costs** (the app suggests amounts from the company rates).
4. Click **Send proposal**: an e-mail in the *Business Trip Approval Request* format goes to the **Director**; the trip appears on your calendar and on MMH Calendar.
:::split-end

## Trip report

:::split.wide crmg_en/trip_report | A trip card: trip details and the Trip report button.
After the trip, click the trip card on the calendar ▸ **Trip report**: record the result of each day and the actual costs. The report is saved in the Business Trip file (Business Trip Report tab) and appears in the weekly report.

Other people's trips are hidden on the calendar; managers switch on **Other people's trips** to see them.
:::split-end

## MMH Calendar

:::split.wide crmg_en/mmhcal | MMH Calendar: the company calendar.
The **MMH Calendar** button on the top bar shows the company calendar (trips, trainings, events, posts). It is read-only; the data comes from Report Hub, Training Hub and the Marketing files. A training session is shown only to its trainer and invited people.
:::split-end
