## Product presentations

:::split crmg_en/present, crmg_en/present_form | Presentation list (left) · a session with the 5 Event review boxes (right).
Left menu ▸ **Presentations · Customer training** ▸ **Presentations** tab.

1. **＋ Add session**: account, date, expected attendees (**Target**), materials folder.
2. After the session: enter **Actual** (real attendees, must be above 0) and the **Event review** in 5 boxes: attendance, summary, customer feedback, sales, next actions.
3. The **detail meter** shows whether the review is complete enough.

A session without Actual or with a missing review box does not count for the KPI yet; a banner at the top lists these sessions.
:::split-end

## Customer training (T&E) [[teams d th]]

:::split.wide crmg_en/te | The Customer training (T&E) tab.
For **Dental VN** and the **two Thailand teams**.

1. **Customer training (T&E)** tab ▸ **＋ Add session**: title, product, place, date, target.
2. **Attending customers**: pick from the contacts / accounts of the session's PIC.
3. After the session: enter Actual + the 5-line review ⇒ **Completed**.

The data is saved in Training Hub (Training Report sheet); for Dental VN it counts for the customer training KPI (C10).
:::split-end

## Monthly Plan

:::split crmg_en/monthly, crmg_en/monthly_drawer | Monthly Plan: the list of items (left) · the PLAN / RESULT editor (right).
Left menu ▸ **Monthly Plan**.

- Each item (by task type) has a **PLAN** and a **RESULT** for the month.
- Click an item ⇒ the editor opens on the right with a suggested template for that item. There is a **Voice** button.
- Closing the editor with unsaved text ⇒ the app asks **Save** / **Discard**.
:::split-end

## Monthly report in Word [[teams d s]]

:::split.wide crmg/word_report | The draft monthly Sales activity report: edit it directly, then create the Word file (Vietnamese interface).
For Dental VN and Surgical VN, using the *MMH Sales activity report* template.

1. **Send report ▸ Monthly report** (or the Monthly Plan page) ▸ **Monthly Word report**.
2. Choose the month and PIC (managers can choose the whole team). The app fills in the numbers: distributor sales, orders, new accounts, field visits, presentations, monthly plan.
3. Edit the draft directly: text, table cells, **＋ Add** rows / tables / sections, delete, reorder, **Undo**. Yellow boxes = parts the salesperson writes.
4. **Create Word file** ⇒ downloads to your device. The draft is kept on the device by month.
:::split-end

## New accounts and new SKUs

:::split crmg_en/newacc, crmg_en/newacc_case | New account cases (left) · a new case (right).
Left menu ▸ **Sales results ▸ New accounts & SKUs**: new account / new SKU cases for KPI **C1** (Dental VN, Surgical VN) and **C2** (Thailand).

1. **＋ Add case**: account, SKU, month, distributor, first order, documents (drag and drop / paste).
2. The app checks the **4 conditions**: in the weekly report · confirmed by the distributor · correct format · first order placed.
3. **Submit** ⇒ the manager chooses **Approve**, **Request changes** (the case returns to draft, documents kept) or **Reject**.

Only the first month counts, up to 130%.
:::split-end
