## How to read this guide

This guide explains how to use **MMH CRM**, from installation to every feature, and **where the data you enter is saved**. Each section is laid out in landscape: **text on the left, pictures on the right**, for both **computer** and **phone**.

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| [[teams vn]] [[teams th]] [[teams d]] … | The feature applies only to the teams shown. No mark ⇒ it applies to every team. |
| [[icon googlesheets]] **File / tab name** | Click to open the Google Sheet that holds the data (you need access to the file). |
| **Illustration** label on a picture | A redrawn browser or phone screen, to help you recognise it. All other pictures are real screenshots of the app with **sample data**. |
| **Yellow frame** on an illustration | Where to tap or click. |

Screenshots use the **English interface of the Thailand teams**. Features that exist only for the Vietnam teams (orders, field photos…) are shown with the Vietnamese interface and marked [[teams vn]].

### The 5 teams using MMH CRM

| Team | Interface | Main differences |
|---|---|---|
| **Dental VN** | Vietnamese | All features: orders, field photos, T&E, monthly Word report |
| **Surgical VN** | Vietnamese | Same as Dental, without T&E |
| **Eyeless VN** | Vietnamese | Same as Surgical; the weekly report does not require next week's plan; no monthly Word report |
| **Thailand Surgical** | English | No orders, no field photos, no trip proposals in the CRM |
| **Thailand Dental** | English | No orders, no field photos; has trip proposals and T&E |
